SERVICES AGREEMENT
Demonstration only. Fictional parties and deliberately inconsistent terms.
1. Parties
This agreement is between Acme Ltd (Client) and Northstar Studio (Supplier).
2. Delivery
Supplier shall deliver the final report within 30 days after receipt of the brief.
3. Delivery schedule
Supplier shall deliver the final report within 45 days after receipt of the brief.
4. Service fee
Client shall pay USD 5,000 for the final report within 15 days after acceptance.
5. Fee schedule
Client shall pay USD 7,500 for the final report within 15 days after acceptance.
6. Confidentiality
Supplier shall not disclose the confidential report to third parties.
7. Distribution
Supplier shall disclose the confidential report to third parties.
8. Review
Client shall review the draft within 7 days after receipt.
9. Termination
Client may terminate this agreement on 30 days written notice if Supplier materially breaches it.
10. Late payment penalty
Client shall pay a late fee of USD 50 if an invoice remains unpaid for 30 days.
11. Milestone
Supplier shall deliver the prototype by December 15, 2026.
12. Separate delivery
Supplier shall deliver the training manual within 60 days after receipt of the brief.
13. Conditional exception
Supplier shall deliver the final report within 60 days if Client requests additional research.
14. Governing terms
The parties intend this document to be a fictional research example. No order of precedence is defined.
